What it does

Synthetic data that survives validation.

01 Fidelity

Statistical properties preserved

Demographics, study status, treatment duration and adverse event rates follow the distributions of the source study, arm by arm.

02 Linkage

Patient-level linkage across domains

Every record in an occurrence dataset belongs to a real subject in ADSL, carries that subject's treatment and sits inside that subject's timeline. This is the part most synthetic data drops, and the part that breaks validation when it does.

03 Checks

Validated on generation

Linkage, key uniqueness, sequence numbering and timing are checked every time data is generated, and failures are reported by record.

04 Output

Ready for pipelines

Reproducible from a seed and written in standard formats, so programmers can test derivations, tables and apps before real data exists.
