What it does

Research that shows its working.

01 Desks

Nine research agents, one stock

Strategy, fundamentals, technical, valuation, news, bull case, bear case, risk and a final report, each run by its own agent and shown at its own desk in a pixel-art office while the research runs.

02 Market

Market context first

Regime, breadth, sector rotation and futures open-interest build-up across the NIFTY 200, with a screener and a stock-in-context view that says whether the market and the sector are behind a name.

03 Signals

Computed, not generated

Levels and 57 rule-based strategies are calculated from price and volume. The language models explain and weigh; they do not invent the numbers.

04 Evidence

Backtests that can say no

Every setup is replayed on the symbol's own history with Indian trading costs (brokerage, STT, exchange fees, GST, stamp duty and slippage), walk-forward folds, an out-of-sample holdout, regime splits and parameter sensitivity. If it lost money there, the verdict refuses to grade it.

05 Derivatives

Futures and options

Expected move to expiry, scenarios with market-implied odds, signals and option structure.

06 Guide

Built to be read

A plain-language guide and a 130-term glossary sit inside the app. Its first lesson: no trade is the normal answer.
